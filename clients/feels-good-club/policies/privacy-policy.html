Privacy policy

This policy explains how Feels Good Club ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit or buy from our store.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, email, delivery address and the products you buy.
  • Payment: card payments are processed securely by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Browsing: basic information about your device and how you use our site (for example pages viewed), collected through cookies.
  • Messages: anything you send us through our contact page, WhatsApp, email or social media.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To contact you about your order or reply to your questions.
  • To improve our store and, if you agree, send you news and offers.
  • To show relevant ads and measure how they perform.
  • To prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share only what's needed with the services that help us run the store: our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment providers, delivery and courier partners, and advertising and analytics providers. We don't sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time. Contact us through our Contact page.

Keeping your information

We keep order information for as long as we need it for our records, accounting and legal reasons, and other information only as long as it's useful for the purposes above.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
