The countries available at checkout are the ones we currently deliver to. If your country isn't listed, we don't ship there yet.

Processing

Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days. Delivery times start once your order has been handed to our courier, and may be longer during holidays or busy sale periods.

Delivery

Please enter your full name, area and a phone number you can be reached on. Our courier will call you to arrange delivery. If the courier can't reach you after several attempts, your order may be returned to us.

Prices and currency

All prices are in US dollars (USD). The delivery fee for your country is shown at checkout before you pay.

Questions

For help with your order or delivery, message us through our Contact page with your order number.
