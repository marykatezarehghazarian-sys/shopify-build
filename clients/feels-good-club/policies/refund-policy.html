Returns & refunds

We want you to love your Feels Good Club order. If something isn't right, here's how we can help.

Damaged, faulty or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item and packaging. We'll send a replacement or give you a full refund, at no extra cost to you.

Changed your mind?

You can return unopened, sealed products within 14 days of delivery. Return shipping is paid by the customer, and the item must reach us in its original condition and packaging.

Items we can't take back

For hygiene and safety reasons, we can't accept returns of opened or used products. This includes toothpaste, whitening strips, pimple patches, nose strips and the Migraine Relief Cap once it has been worn, unless the item arrived damaged or faulty.

How refunds are paid

  • Card payments: refunded to the original card within 5–10 business days after we approve the return.
  • Cash on delivery: refunded by bank transfer or money transfer service, as agreed with you.

Delivery fees are refunded only when the item arrived damaged, faulty or wrong.

How to start a return

Message us through our Contact page with your order number, the item, and the reason for the return. We'll reply with the next steps.
